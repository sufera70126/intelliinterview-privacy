# Privacy Policy – IntelliInterview

**Last Updated:** 30 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

IntelliInterview is an academic project developed for educational purposes. It is designed to help students and job seekers practise interviews and improve their interview skills.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected and how it may be used when using IntelliInterview.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, IntelliInterview may collect:

- **Basic account information:** Name and email address.
- **LinkedIn profile information:** Basic profile details made available through LinkedIn's authorised sign-in service, such as name, profile picture and email address, where permitted.
- **Resume information:** Resume files uploaded by users and text extracted from those files.
- **Interview information:** Practice answers, interview responses, scores and feedback generated by the system.

## 3. How Information Is Used

The information may be used to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Display basic LinkedIn profile details after authorised sign-in.
- Analyse uploaded resumes.
- Provide interview questions and practice activities.
- Evaluate practice answers and provide feedback.
- Display interview performance and progress.

## 4. LinkedIn Sign-In

If a user chooses to connect LinkedIn, IntelliInterview may request permission to access basic profile information through LinkedIn's authorised authentication process.

IntelliInterview does not claim access to private LinkedIn information, complete work history, education records or other restricted profile data unless LinkedIn explicitly makes such information available and the user authorises access.

Use of LinkedIn is also subject to LinkedIn's own terms and privacy policy.

## 5. Data Storage and Sharing

Information may be processed and stored by the IntelliInterview application to provide its features.

IntelliInterview does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information should not be shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the application, comply with legal requirements or where the user has given permission.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide the application's features and support its academic demonstration.

Users who want to request access to or deletion of their information can contact the project team using the contact details provided below.

## 7. Data Security

Reasonable measures will be taken to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid uploading confidential or highly sensitive information.

## 8. Children's Privacy

IntelliInterview is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the IntelliInterview project develops. Any changes will be published on this page.
